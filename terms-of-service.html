1. What MessMate does

MessMate lets you record members, meals, bazar (grocery) expenses, bills, deposits and monthly results for a shared mess or household, and works out the meal rate and each person's balance. The app works offline. An optional Share with members feature lets a manager show the accounts on members' phones. How we handle data is explained in our Privacy Policy.
